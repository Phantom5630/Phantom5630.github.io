GAME INTRODUCTION

It's Just Level One

3D Platformer | Trolling

A "classic" 3D platformer where you find your way to the end point.
It is mainly inspired by Cat Mario. It is designed to frustrate and annoy the player given the only theme of Heartbreak. The forms of fun aimed for the level are thrill of danger as well as advancement and completion.
Disclaimer: This is just a gameplay level demo, not a full game.

WHAT I HAVE DONE

Misleading Color Coding

Misleading Color-Coded Platform

  • Purposely set green as the right color at beginning.
  • Break the rule of "green is right" at the end of section when player have certain level of trust towards it.
  • Indirectly warn the player that things in the level are untrustworthy.

Hidden Platform

Hidden Platform

  • Hidden platform placed right at the positions that may block the player when jumping to another platform.
  • The player can either avoid or use them to proceed.
  • Provide some thrill of danger and potentially make the player jump in place more often for the purpose of "mine-sweeping".

Dodging Platform

Dodging Platform

  • Platforms that move to fixed position quickly at the moment player enter its range.
  • Provide more variation of platform mechanics for the player to memorize while proceeding.

Dialogue

Misleading Lines

Passive Agressive Line

  • Misleading lines telling the player to wait for the door to be opened while he actually need to run away from the "doors" below first.
  • Passive agressive lines mocking the player for taking too many trials in the level.
